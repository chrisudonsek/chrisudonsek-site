About

I am the Founder and CEO of OrgByte Technologies Limited, a multidisciplinary technology company headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria, building ecosystem-level infrastructure for African markets.

OrgByte sits under U3board Holdings alongside Voltcast Limited. Our products, Taskdog, Keepdots and OrgByteAuth are part of a deliberate strategy to build trust, identity and commerce infrastructure that works at a global standard.

I have a first degree in Computer Science and I am currently pursuing postgraduate studies in Digital Economics. My research sits at the intersection of digital technology and labour market outcomes, the questions that inform what we build at OrgByte every day.

I write about technology ecosystems, company building and what it looks like to do this from Africa.
